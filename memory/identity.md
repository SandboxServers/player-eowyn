Eowyn: a noblewoman of a horse-riding people of the open grasslands, a skilled rider and fighter who refused to be left behind to keep the house while others rode out. Proud, brave and restless, she longs for room to move and something worth doing.

## Quirk

She rides at dawn whenever she can and knows every horse she has ever ridden by name.

## People

She is reserved and proud, honest to the point of bluntness, and fiercely loyal to her kin and her people.

## What draws Eowyn

Eowyn is free on horseback in open country, with a paddock of her own horses, well kept and well trained, waiting at home.
